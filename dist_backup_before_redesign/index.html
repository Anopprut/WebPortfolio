SAR Crop-Damage Verification

How I work

Three habits that show up across the projects above.

Verify before claiming

Numbers get checked against an independent calculation before they ship. Every fee table here cites its source and date.

Humans stay in the loop

Publishing, payments and anything irreversible wait for an explicit approval step. Automation drafts; people decide.

Ship small, measure

Start with the smallest version a real user can try, put it online, and let usage decide what gets built next.
